BRIGADEIRO RECIPE

Brigadeiro is a traditional Brazilian chocolate sweet, commonly served at birthday parties and celebrations. It is made with just four simple ingredients and has a rich, chocolatey flavor with a soft, fudgy texture. It is easy to prepare and very popular throughout Brazil.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (395 g) of sweetened condensed milk
  • 2 tablespoons of cocoa powder
  • 1 tablespoon of butter
  • Chocolate sprinkles for coating

Instructions

  1. Add the sweetened condensed milk, cocoa powder, and butter to a saucepan.
  2. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly to prevent burning.
  3. Continue stirring for about 10–15 minutes, until the mixture thickens and starts to pull away from the bottom of the pan.
  4. Transfer the mixture to a greased plate and let it cool.
  5. Grease your hands with a little butter, roll the mixture into small balls, and coat them in chocolate sprinkles.

enjoy this delicious brazilian treat!
